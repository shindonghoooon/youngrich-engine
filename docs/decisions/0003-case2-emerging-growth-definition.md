# ADR-0003: Define Case 2 as Emerging / Asymmetric Growth

Status: ACCEPTED

Date: 2026-09-04

## Context

“Loss-making growth” was too broad and mixed pre-commercial speculation with companies
showing real customer adoption and emerging economics.

## Decision

Case 2 requires actual commercial revenue/adoption and evaluates growth, gross profit,
cash burn, runway, dilution, and per-share growth.

## Why

This retains early winners without treating revenue growth alone as sufficient.

## Alternatives Considered

Generic loss-making-growth classification; Case 1 profitability rules.

## Consequences

Pre-commercial companies remain unresolved/edge cases; funding risk stays first-class.
